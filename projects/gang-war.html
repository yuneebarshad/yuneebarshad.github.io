Overview

ROLE: Senior Game Developer — Gameplay Architecture, Progression, AI Integration & Monetization

Gang Mafia Shooting Crime Game is a production Android and iOS third-person action shooter built around gang territory control and objective-driven combat. Players create a gang, fight through rival-controlled city areas, complete attack, survival, capture, boss and vehicle objectives, recruit companions, upgrade weapons and capture territory through a persistent map-based campaign. I designed and implemented the project-specific territory campaign, reusable mission and enemy-wave architecture, weapon purchasing and upgrades, gang-member recruitment, economy, hybrid JSON and PlayerPrefs persistence, Addressable runtime content flow, AppLovin MAX monetization, Firebase Analytics and mobile release preparation.

⚙

Framework note: Invector provided the baseline third-person controller, shooter and AI foundations. I customized its combat and camera behaviour, then built the project-specific progression, missions, wave orchestration, weapon economy, gang recruitment, persistence, monetization, analytics and release systems around that foundation.

Gameplay Loop

Select mission→ Fight enemy waves→ Complete objective→ Earn XP & cash→ Upgrade & recruit→ Capture territory

Key Highlights

  • Designed a data-driven territory campaign with 11 missions across three rival gangs, including attack, survival, capture, boss and vehicle-based objectives.
  • Extended Invector’s shooter foundation with custom target detection, aim-state feedback, reload orchestration, ammunition onboarding, runtime weapon-stat injection, camera/FOV firing feedback and HUD integration.
  • Built reusable enemy-wave orchestration that loads enemies through local Addressables and assigns health, damage, accuracy, weapons, combat distance, cover settings and Invector FSM behaviour from configurable profiles.
  • Implemented six active upgradeable weapons across SMG, assault-rifle and shotgun categories, with six progression tiers controlling damage, magazine capacity, reload time, fire interval, accuracy and upgrade cost.
  • Built persistent gang-member recruitment with level-gated unlocks, companion spawning and individual health interfaces.
  • Implemented hybrid local persistence using JSON for structured territory, weapon and gang-member state, with PlayerPrefs for cash, XP, health, selections and onboarding flags.
  • Integrated Unity Addressables, RCC vehicle mechanics, AppLovin MAX and Firebase Analytics, and prepared production releases for Android and iOS.

Core Systems

✓ Invector Combat & Camera Customization (Framework Extension + Script)

Extended Invector’s controller, shooter and AI foundations with the project-specific combat behaviour and presentation required by the game.

  • Implemented camera-based target raycasting and custom aim-state feedback
  • Coordinated aim-in, aim-out, firing and reload presentation with the combat flow
  • Implemented ammunition capacity, pickups, onboarding and reload control
  • Injected purchased weapon statistics into the active Invector weapon at runtime
  • Added camera position and FOV feedback during firing and connected combat state with the HUD
  • Integrated enemy ragdoll and hit-direction support with the damage pipeline
✓ Territory Campaign & Mission Progression (Script + ScriptableObjects)

Built a reusable mission architecture and used it to design and implement 11 campaign missions across three rival-gang territories.

  • Designed and implemented 11 campaign missions across three rival-gang territories
  • Implemented locked, active and completed mission states with cross-territory unlocking after boss completion
  • Configured attack, survival, capture, boss and vehicle-based mission structures
  • Connected mission rewards, cash, XP and territory ownership with the progression flow
  • Implemented capture missions where completed combat objectives activate the final capture location
  • Integrated RCC vehicle health, collision damage and mission completion into vehicle objectives
✓ Enemy AI & Wave Orchestration (Script + AI FSM)

Built a reusable encounter layer around local Addressables and Invector FSM AI so mission content could change through configuration rather than duplicated controllers.

  • Loads enemy and boss prefabs through local Unity Addressables
  • Controls wave timing, spawn points, survival conditions and boss-wave completion
  • Assigns health, damage, accuracy, reload timing, weapon type and combat range at runtime
  • Applies configurable cover distance, navigation and Invector FSM behaviour profiles
  • Supports difficulty variation through reusable enemy skill-profile assets
  • Coordinates enemy completion, health-pickup spawning and progression callbacks
✓ Weapon Progression & Economy (Script + Addressables)

Implemented a configurable purchase, equipment and upgrade system connected directly to the active combat weapon.

  • Configured six active progression weapons across SMG, assault-rifle and shotgun categories
  • Implemented purchase, equip, switch and upgrade states
  • Built six upgrade tiers per weapon for damage, capacity, reload time, fire interval and accuracy
  • Connected upgrade pricing with the game economy and mission rewards
  • Loads purchased player weapons through local Addressables at runtime
  • Persists ownership, equipped state and upgrade progress through structured JSON data
✓ Gang Recruitment & Companion System (Script + AI Integration)

Built a persistent companion system that connects progression-gated recruitment with runtime combat support.

  • Implemented three recruitable gang members with level-gated unlock requirements
  • Stores unlocked and recruited states across sessions
  • Spawns selected companions into supported combat missions
  • Connects companion damage and accuracy values with runtime combat behaviour
  • Provides independent health interfaces for active gang members
✓ Hybrid JSON & PlayerPrefs Persistence (Script)

Used a hybrid local persistence architecture that separates structured progression data from lightweight scalar values.

  • Stores mission completion and lock states in structured level JSON data
  • Stores weapon ownership, upgrades and equipped status in dedicated weapon JSON data
  • Stores gang-member unlock and recruitment states in dedicated recruitment JSON data
  • Uses PlayerPrefs for gang name, cash, XP, player health, onboarding flags and selected indices
  • Restores persistent manager state during initialization and across scene changes
  • Maintains default first-launch values and existing player progression
✓ Unity Addressables & Mobile UI (Script + UI)

Used local Unity Addressables to keep runtime UI and gameplay dependencies modular without claiming remote content delivery.

  • Loads map, weapon selection and pause screens at runtime
  • Loads level-complete, failure, boss-eliminated and capture-result interfaces through the same flow
  • Instantiates and releases player weapons, enemy archetypes and boss prefabs on demand
  • Reduces direct scene references to runtime-created interface and gameplay assets
  • Built mobile interfaces with uGUI and TextMeshPro for map, progression, recruitment and combat flow
✓ Monetization & Firebase Analytics (Third-Party Integration + Script)

Integrated production advertising flows with validated gameplay rewards and impression-level revenue analytics.

  • Integrated AppLovin MAX banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements
  • Implemented interstitial frequency controls and rewarded-ad completion validation
  • Connected rewarded flows with double rewards, cash recovery and supported progression actions
  • Implemented loading states and exponential retry handling for failed ad requests
  • Restores audio, joystick input and gameplay callbacks after advertisement completion
  • Logs impression-level revenue to Firebase Analytics with network, format, ad unit, revenue and currency data
